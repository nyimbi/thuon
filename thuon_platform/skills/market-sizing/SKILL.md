# Market Sizing Skill

## Core rule
Never trust one estimate. Produce three independent estimates and reconcile at the end.

## Method

### Top-down
Start with industry analyst figure or proxy (e.g., "data center software" \$X B,
segment share %, growth rate g). Discount to actual addressable portion (geography,
segment, product fit).

### Bottom-up
target_size (units) × penetration % × price × frequency.
Triangulate the target-size number from 2–3 independent sources; if they disagree
by >2×, the sizing is not grounded — say so.

### Value theory
Value created (e saved cost, revenue enabled) vs. willingness-to-pay.

### Triangulation
- Inputs within 10× → range estimate with confidence band.
- Inputs >10× apart → identify which is most authoritative, justify, and drop
  the others rather than average.

## Verification
- Sanity check: SAM should never exceed TAM. SOM in year 1 shouldn't exceed
  1–3% of SAM.
- Source labels [A]/[B]/[C] on every input.
- Explicit assumption list.
- "What would invalidate this."

Output: numbers Table, Reasoning, Confidence, Assumptions, Falsifiers.
