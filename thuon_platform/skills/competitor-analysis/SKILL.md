# Competitor Analysis Skill

## Method

1. **Universe**: list 5–8 real competitors; group direct, indirect, substitute.
2. **Dimensions** (score 1–5):
   - product breadth/depth
   - pricing & packaging
   - distribution channels
   - customer-lock-in / switching cost (moat)
   - technology advantage
   - brand/trust
3. **Per competitor card**: one paragraph, sourced (grade), plus one quote
   from their website/docs (grade B at best).
4. **Differentiation thesis**: two sentences. State what we do better, what
   we don't, and what's defensible in 18 months.

## Anti-patterns

- Never call a competitor "the dominant player" without a metric.
- Never score on vague axis ("innovation").
- SWOT differences must be evidenced, not listed for completeness.

## Output

- Competitive landscape table (dimension scores + overall)
- Per-competitor cards
- Differentiation thesis (two sentences)
- Recommended positioning shift (if any)
